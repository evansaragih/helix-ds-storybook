## What it is / when to use it

A horizontal track showing determinate completion percentage. Use for upload/download progress, onboarding steps, or form completion. Do not use for an indeterminate/unknown-duration wait — use Spinner instead.

**Code:** `ProgressBar from helix-design-system`
