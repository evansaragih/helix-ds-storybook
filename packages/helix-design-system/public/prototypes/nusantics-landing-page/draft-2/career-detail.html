Technical Account Manager

Your Exciting Role

  • Lead the Technical Sales team and own account goals and customer satisfaction.
  • Set account strategy for retention, growth, and expansion.
  • Oversee technical consultations on shrimp farming, products, lab work, and sequencing.
  • Align with Commercial on targets and cross-selling opportunities.
  • Monitor account performance, CSAT, and risks.
  • Set team KPIs, work plans, and reporting standards.
  • Run field trials, sampling, and customer projects.
  • Analyze customer feedback and field data to make data-driven recommendations.
  • Handle escalations and coordinate cross-functional fixes.
  • Build stakeholder relationships and run periodic account reviews.

Ideally, You Would Have

  • Bachelor's degree in aquaculture, fisheries, marine science, biology, life sciences, or a related field.
  • 3–5 years of experience; team leadership experience preferred.
  • Track record in account management and customer retention.
  • Knowledge of shrimp farming and aquaculture; lab, molecular, or sequencing background is a plus.
  • Strong leadership, communication, and coordination skills.
  • Analytical and problem-solving mindset.
  • Proficient in Google Sheets or Excel; Metabase is a plus.
  • Customer-focused, with experience handling escalations.
  • Able to coordinate cross-functional projects.
  • Willing to do field visits and travel.
