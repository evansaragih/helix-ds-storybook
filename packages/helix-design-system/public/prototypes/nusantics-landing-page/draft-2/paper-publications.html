Urinary detection of high-risk HPV DNA to enhance cervical cancer screening in developing countries

To increase cervical cancer screening capacity and participation, we evaluated the performance of an hrHPV qPCR kit on urine samples compared with cervical swabs. The swab samples showed a sensitivity of 96.55% and a specificity of 99.87%, while urine samples showed a sensitivity of 80.88% and a specificity of 100%, supporting urine as a less invasive option for screening in developing countries.
August 30, 2024

Comparative analysis of PathoScan hrHPV qPCR kits with four- and five-channel qPCR methods for the identification of high-risk human papillomaviruses

PathoScan hrHPV Lite qPCR kit displayed excellent diagnostic accuracy and good agreement with the five-channel method. The kit was developed as an alternative needing only a 4-channel qPCR instrument, and was compared with the 5-channel kit on 96 cervical swab samples. Both sensitivity and specificity reached 100%, and the Bland-Altman analysis showed good agreement between the two methods.
September 20, 2022

Performance of saline and water gargling for SARS-CoV-2 reverse transcriptase PCR testing: a systematic review and meta-analysis

This review systematically assessed the performance of saline and water gargling for SARS-CoV-2 detection by reverse transcriptase PCR. Gargle samples were evaluated both for diagnosis and for monitoring viral shedding, as a less invasive and more comfortable alternative to nasopharyngeal swabs that could ease sample collection and widen access to testing in laboratories and communities.
August 8, 2022

The efficiency of selective pooling strategy in a COVID-19 diagnostic laboratory

Pooling strategy could reduce personnel workload and reagent cost, and increase laboratory capacity by up to 50%. A 4-sample pooling method was applied in a diagnostic laboratory in Semarang on 15,216 specimens in 3,804 pools, of which 26.47% were positive. A positivity cutoff of 22% or lower in the tested population saves at least 50% of the reactions.
July 29, 2022

Real-time RT-PCR Ct value is not associated with COVID-19 disease severity: an observational study in tertiary COVID-19 referral hospital of West Java, Indonesia

Although Ct value reflects viral load, its role is concluded to be clinically insignificant in determining COVID-19 disease severity. We analyzed 207 patients treated at Dr. Hasan Sadikin Hospital and compared real-time RT-PCR Ct values across severity groups. No significant difference in Ct value was found between the groups in the second week of illness.
